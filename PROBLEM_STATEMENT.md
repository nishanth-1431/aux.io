# AUX.io — Problem Statement

Many online music and audio platforms are becoming increasingly focused on advertisements, paid subscriptions, and additional features that can make simple music listening less accessible and less distraction-free for users.

**aux.io** aims to provide a free and open-source alternative that offers users a simple, seamless, and ad-free music listening experience. The platform will provide access to openly available music while also allowing independent authors to submit their own music for publication.

To maintain the quality and legality of the platform's content, submitted music will undergo a review process. Only music approved by an authorized reviewer will become available to users.

The goal of **aux.io** is to create a simple and accessible music platform while providing a practical project for understanding how a real-world Java web application is designed, developed, and deployed.
